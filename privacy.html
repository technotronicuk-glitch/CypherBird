Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Zero-Log Policy

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. Our systems are intentionally engineered to be entirely private by design. We explicitly avoid tracking IP addresses, browser fingerprints, or user session metadata.

2. Data Security & Encryption

All communication and data transfer pipelines are secured using industry-standard AES-256 and RSA-4096 cryptographic protocols. Decryption keys remain exclusively on your local hardware. Because we do not store these keys, we are unable to recover data on your behalf.

3. Third-Party Interactions

Our platform operates entirely free of third-party trackers, cookies, or marketing analytics suites. Any localized AI interactions are processed through self-contained instances that do not transmit data back to external networks or centralized machine learning datasets.

Our Data Sovereignty Commitment

We believe individual digital privacy is fundamental. Because our technical architecture is built on a strict zero-knowledge model, we physically do not possess the capacity to aggregate, sell, or surrender your data to any external entities.
